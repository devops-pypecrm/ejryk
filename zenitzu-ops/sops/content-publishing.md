# SOP: publishing content

1. Draft with the `content-creator` agent. Save to `outputs/blog/` or `outputs/social/`.
2. Human review: facts, tone (`context/brand-voice.md`), links, no invented numbers.
3. Add images from `context/style-guide.md` guidelines.
4. Publish, then record the URL in the task's Output field.
5. Re-check performance after 14 days and note learnings in `outputs/reports/`.
