# SOP: handling a new lead

1. New enquiries appear in **Admin > Leads** with status `new`.
2. **Within 1 working hour:** reply on WhatsApp (preferred) or email. Say who we are, repeat their need in one line, propose a 15-minute call.
3. Set the status to `contacted` and add a note with the time and what was agreed.
4. After the call: send a proposal (use `templates/proposal.md` and the `proposal-writer` skill). Save it in `outputs/proposals/`.
5. Follow up after 2 days, then after 7 days. Then mark `lost` with a reason in notes.
6. When they sign: mark `won`, create a task per deliverable in **Admin > Tasks**.
