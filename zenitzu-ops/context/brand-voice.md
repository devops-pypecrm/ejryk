# Brand voice

**Personality:** fast, clear, confident, friendly. A sharp engineer who explains things like a good teacher.

## Do
- Lead with the outcome and the time: "Live in one week", not "We offer web development services".
- Use short sentences, plain words, active voice. One idea per sentence.
- Be specific: numbers, timelines, deliverables.
- Use the lightning/speed idea sparingly (once per page, not every line).

## Don't
- No jargon without a plain explanation. No hype words (revolutionary, world-class, cutting-edge, synergy).
- No guarantees we cannot back up. No fake urgency.
- No em-dash chains or long paragraphs.

## Examples
- Yes: "Got an idea? We can make it live in one week."
- No: "We leverage cutting-edge technologies to deliver best-in-class digital solutions."
