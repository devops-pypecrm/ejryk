# Visual style guide

- **Colours:** ink `#07041c`, indigo `#5a34d0`, violet `#8b6cff`, lilac `#c39bff`, lightning yellow `#f2c935`. Gradient: violet > lilac > yellow.
- **Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (headings 600, body 400).
- **Look:** clean, high contrast, lots of white space, big type, thin lines, one yellow highlight per layout.
- **Motifs:** the lightning bolt mark, thin bolt-jag dividers, the kanji 雷 as a faint accent. Never use character art or third-party anime imagery.
- **Photos:** real, natural stock photography; no obviously AI-generated faces.
- **Social creatives:** 1080x1350 carousels, max 25 words per slide, one idea per slide, CTA on the last slide.
- **Logo:** yellow bolt in a dark square + "zenitzu" with "TECH SOLUTIONS" beneath.
