# Social posts

1. Read `context/brand-voice.md` and `context/style-guide.md`.
2. Produce for each idea: hook (under 12 words), body, CTA, 5 relevant hashtags.
3. Carousels: 5-7 slides, max 25 words per slide, one idea per slide, CTA on the last.
4. Give a visual direction note per post for the `designer` agent.
5. Save to `outputs/social/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`.
