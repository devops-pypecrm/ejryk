# Blog writer

1. Read `context/brand-voice.md`, `context/audience.md`, `templates/blog-post.md`.
2. Confirm the keyword and the reader's question. If missing, propose three and pick one.
3. Open with a 2-3 sentence direct answer. Then table of contents, sections as questions, an FAQ, and a call to action to /contact.
4. 900-1,400 words, short paragraphs, bullets where useful. No invented statistics; cite or leave out.
5. Save to `outputs/blog/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.
